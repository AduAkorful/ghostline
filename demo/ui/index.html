The payment rail

A channel carries the small stuff.

The payer locks a deposit once. Signed vouchers keep a running total until one side closes the channel.

  1. 01

    Open a channel

    The payer locks a deposit under the payer, service, and expiry conditions.

    →
  2. 02

    Receive a quote

    The service returns a price and the terms for the requested call.

    →
  3. 03

    Pay off-chain

    The payer signs a cumulative voucher. The service checks and records the new total.

    →
  4. 04

    Settle once

    The service submits the latest accepted voucher; the contract pays the total and returns the remainder by the channel rules.

    ✓

↳ If the channel reaches its expiry without settlement, the payer can use the refund path to reclaim the unredeemed funds.

Privacy boundary

Private from observers.
Visible to the service.

Ghostline hides the spending pattern from third parties. It does not hide activity from the service receiving payment.

01 / PAYER

Your full view

You know your calls, channel details, and the vouchers you sign.

02 / SERVICE

Each request

The service sees its requests, prices, and voucher stream as it serves them.

03 / OBSERVER

One close record

Observers do not see each call through the channel. Settlement reveals the amount and branch; fees remain public.

! Funding patterns, timing, and who pays fees can still create correlations. Ghostline does not promise anonymity or unlinkable funding.

Know the limits

Early development.
Testnet only.

  • Ghostline is unaudited. Do not use real funds.
  • Fees are public and may exceed the value of small calls.
  • Settlement reveals the amount and executed branch.
  • Call counts are service records, not chain-attested receipts.
