Ghostline / Esmeralda quickstart

YOUR WALLET · YOUR DEPLOYMENT

Open once.
Pay per call.

Use Ghostline with your own Esmeralda wallet, provider terms, and fee settings. This guide outlines the CLI path from channel setup to one verified settlement.

Unaudited, testnet software. Ghostline is early development and for Esmeralda only. Do not use real funds. Examples are templates; they contain no valid deployment identifiers or credentials.

00 / BEFORE YOU START

Gather real deployment terms.

You need a compatible wallet daemon, the deployed Ghostline template, and a service that accepts the channel terms. Nothing here creates a hosted service or shared wallet.

Wallets and funds

Use a loopback Ootle wallet daemon, a funded opener account, and a funded service fee account. The opener pays the deposit and opening fee; the service fee account pays settlement fees.

Provider parameters

Get the real indexer URL, network tag, template hash, service public key, price, and accepted expiry range from your provider. Never copy placeholder values as live identifiers.

Private keys

The payer key signs vouchers. The opener wallet's view-only key at index 0 unblinds the channel output. They are separate secrets; keep both out of shell history and shared MCP configuration.

Service signer

The service signer and the fee-account signer have different roles. Use the correct wallet-local KeyId reference for each operation.

Keep the wallet daemon on loopback. Ghostline uses its local wallet daemon's None authentication flow, which grants wallet administration access. Do not expose that endpoint publicly.

01 / PREPARE

Create private channel state.

Use an owner-only directory. Generate a payer signing key into a private file; use only its public value in channel parameters and provide its secret through the signing process's private environment.

PRIVATE WORKSPACE AND PAYER KEY
umask 077
mkdir -p /secure/path
ghostline keygen > /secure/path/payer-key.txt

Choose a future expiry epoch and a unique 32-byte nonce. Compute the payer root from the exact channel terms, then initialize the channel record using the provider's real values:

COMPUTE CHANNEL TERMS · REPLACE PLACEHOLDERS
ghostline spec \
  --network-tag <esmeralda-network-tag> \
  --deposit 100000 \
  --payer-hex <payer-public-key-hex> \
  --service-hex <service-public-key-hex> \
  --expiry-epoch <future-epoch> \
  --nonce-hex <unique-32-byte-nonce-hex> \
  --template-hex <deployed-template-hash-hex>

100000 means 0.1 tTARI in integer micro-units. Fees are additional. The full channel init command also needs your wallet accounts, indexer, ledger/session paths, and maximum accepted epoch. See the detailed source guide for every setup flag and troubleshooting note.

02 / SERVICE

Start the other side.

Run the reference API locally, or use a compatible provider. The service sets the price and accepted expiry range. Its ledger and the payer session must stay in separate private files.

REFERENCE SERVICE · 10 µT PER CALL
ghostline-paid-api init --ledger /secure/service-ledger.json --session /secure/service-session.json <channel-parameters>
ghostline-paid-api serve --bind 127.0.0.1:8787 --ledger /secure/service-ledger.json --price 10 <service-terms>

The angle-bracket groups above are explanatory placeholders, not CLI flags. Use the complete, current flags in the detailed quickstart when configuring the reference service.

03 / OPEN AND PAY

Review first. Then pay off-chain.

Supply the payer and claim secrets privately to the process. Dry-run the channel open, inspect its fee estimate and required funds, then open with a reviewed positive fee cap.

OPEN ONLY AFTER REVIEW
ghostline channel open --file /secure/path/channel.json --dry-run
ghostline channel open --file /secure/path/channel.json --max-fee <reviewed-opening-fee-cap>
ghostline channel status --file /secure/path/channel.json

Once open, pay for ten calls at 10 µT each. The policy budget limits voucher spending, not transaction fees.

MAKE TEN PAID CALLS
ghostline-paid-api call \
  --url http://127.0.0.1:8787/fortune \
  --session /secure/path/session.json \
  --calls 10 \
  --vendor-hex <service-public-key-hex> \
  --budget 100 \
  --max-per-call 10

Each call signs the next cumulative voucher total. Calls do not submit individual transactions to the chain. The service records accepted calls; that record alone is not proof of settlement.

04 / SETTLE AND VERIFY

Submit one aggregate total.

Stop paying on this channel. Dry-run settlement and review the fee before choosing to submit. The channel-file shortcut has a fixed maximum fee of 100,000 µT; use it only if that cap is acceptable for your setup.

CHANNEL-FILE SETTLEMENT · FIXED FEE CAP
ghostline channel settle --file /secure/path/channel.json --dry-run
# Submit only after reviewing the result and fee cap:
ghostline channel settle --file /secure/path/channel.json --submit
CHECK INDEXER FINALITY
ghostline indexer result --url <esmeralda-indexer-url> --transaction-id <settlement-transaction-id>

Confirm Finalized Commit. The indexer confirms the settlement and amount; the service ledger supplies the call count. Those are separate records.

ALTERNATIVE / REFUND

Refund after expiry.

If the channel remains unspent through expiry, the payer can use the refund branch. This cannot follow a successful settlement. The channel-file refund helper also uses a fixed maximum fee of 100,000 µT and needs the payer fee account and payer signing key.

EXPIRED, UNSPENT OUTPUT ONLY
ghostline channel refund --file /secure/path/channel.json --dry-run
# Submit only after confirming expiry, output state, and fee cap:
ghostline channel refund --file /secure/path/channel.json --submit
